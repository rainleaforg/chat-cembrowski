BAPa is built on Dr. George Cembrowski's research in laboratory quality management — work on
Sequential Difference Deviation (SDD) analysis, patient-based quality control, Westgard rules and
control charting, delta checks, and related statistical methods for detecting and quantifying
preanalytic and analytic error in clinical laboratory testing. Jenna Rimkus contributed research on
biological, analytical, and pre-analytical variation that helped shape the tool built on top of
that work.

The published papers and conference posters behind BAPa are browsable at `/presentation`. Topics
span laboratory instruments and specimens (troponin assays, blood gas analyzers, hematology
analyzers, blood drawing tubes) and laboratory operations and statistics (quality control rules,
proficiency testing, the testing process, test utilization). The methodology has been validated
against real hospital data from Edmonton laboratories, and the results are published rather than
proprietary.

When answering a question about a specific paper, poster, or finding, cite it — don't just say
research exists on the topic without pointing to it.
