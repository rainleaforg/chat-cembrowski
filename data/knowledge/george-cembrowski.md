Dr. George Cembrowski is a Clinical Professor in the Department of Laboratory Medicine and
Pathology at the University of Alberta, and a recognized authority in laboratory quality
management, with decades of work spanning clinical practice, expert advisory panels, industry
consulting, and publication. BAPa is built on his research.

## Education

- PhD, University of Wisconsin
- MD, University of Calgary

## Positions

- Clinical Professor, Department of Laboratory Medicine and Pathology, University of Alberta
- Former Director of Medical Biochemistry, University of Alberta Hospital, Edmonton
- Former Laboratory Director, Park Nicollet Clinic, Minneapolis

## Advisory and consulting

- Expert panel member for the Centers for Disease Control
- Expert panel member for the College of American Pathologists
- Consultant to in vitro diagnostic companies
- Consultant to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration

## Publications and speaking

- Author of *Laboratory Quality Management*
- Author of numerous scientific and technical publications
- A sought-after speaker on laboratory quality across the United States and Canada

## Role in BAPa

BAPa grew out of Dr. Cembrowski's research, developed together with Jenna Rimkus, and was designed
to make the methods behind that research usable by laboratories on their own data through an
accessible, user-friendly platform.
