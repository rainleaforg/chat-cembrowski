Jenna Rimkus is an undergraduate Geomatics Engineering student at the University of Calgary and a
co-creator of BAPa. Her research focuses on biological, analytical, and pre-analytical variation —
the same three sources of variation BAPa's name comes from — combining statistical analysis with
software development to improve how clinical laboratory data is interpreted.

## Education

- Undergraduate student, Geomatics Engineering, University of Calgary
- Recipient of the Seymour Schulich Academic Excellence Entrance Scholarship

## Research focus

- Biological, analytical, and pre-analytical variation
- Statistical analysis of clinical laboratory data
- Software development applied to laboratory data interpretation

## Recognition

- Seymour Schulich Academic Excellence Entrance Scholarship
- Cardiovascular Health Division Abstract Award
- ADLM Invitational ePoster Discussion (invitation-only session)

## Role in BAPa

BAPa grew out of the research of Dr. George Cembrowski and Jenna Rimkus together, and was designed
to make the methods behind that research usable by laboratories on their own data through an
accessible, user-friendly platform. Her work on variation analysis, paired with software
development, is what turned that research into the tool.
