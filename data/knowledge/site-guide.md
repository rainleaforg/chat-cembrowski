BAPa's site (`/`) has these sections, in the main navigation:

- **App** (`/`, `/tool`) — the landing page and the analysis tool itself. Requires sign-in and
  administrator-approved access; see the access management documentation.
- **Research** (`/research`) — this research chat, open to anyone with no sign-in. It can answer
  questions about BAPa, its research basis, the site, and the analysis tool; general medical
  questions using MedlinePlus and PubMed; and general questions outside those topics. It gives
  general information, not medical advice, and does not diagnose or treat.
- **Presentations** (`/presentation`) — the published papers and conference posters behind BAPa.
- **Interview** (`/interview`) — a video interview.
- **About Us** (`/about-us`) — biographies of Dr. George Cembrowski and Jenna Rimkus.
- **Documentation** (`/docs`) — security and privacy documentation, and access management.

A few `/research` sub-pages (`/research/background`, `/methodology`, `/results`, `/citations`) are
placeholders with no content yet. Don't cite them as sources or claim they already explain
something — point to `/presentation` and the documentation pages instead, which are complete.
